# Surface: home (index.html)

Scope: the whole one-page site (hero → about → photos → research → footer); Imprint, Privacy and 404 inherit the frame. Visitor mode: Read. Audience, content and constraints live in PRODUCT.md.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is the shop drawing for something being built, reduced to its cut list. The hand-drawn sign is the one painted plate; real crayon taken from it (the swatches and the sign's own border line as section openers) is the only other drawn material, and everything else is graphite linework on drafting film. Refuses the academic template (serif name, cream, bullet publication list) and the corporate claim-and-proof page.

OWN-WORLD: Cool drafting-film ground (#f3f5f3) carrying a very subtle paper grain (user request, to match the crayon), graphite ink, no grid paper, no dimension lines, no title block. Pens: 2px ink (icon buttons, toggles), 1px hair (row dividers, photo frames), and real crayon strokes as section openers (user: "some crayon lines"): the sign scan's own bottom border line, cropped with its paper knocked out; above About and the legal titles in its own black, above Scalable Governance and AI in research refilled with the sign's real blue and pink crayon fills (user: "I like the color"), running just past the content column (user: "just slightly longer than the actual page content") at a delicate 8px (user: "a bit more delicate"). Swatches carry frames cut from the same real line (user: crayon borders). Abstracts run to 38em (user: "use the full width to the right a bit more"), a deliberate step past the 34em reading measure. Each section is a sheet: label in a narrow left column, text in the wide right column (user: text blocks "feel lost"). Papers carry no invented numbers (user); each row starts with a small swatch cropped from the sign's real crayon fills, which brings colour into Research (user: "a little more color"). Opened papers set details in the left column and the abstract across the right (user: "use the horizontal space better"). The footer is a full-width band in a deeper paper tone under one thin 1px line (user: "full screen width, narrower line"). Narrow drafting caps (Archivo Narrow) for labels and venues; a plain grotesk (Hanken Grotesk) for reading. Sign colour appears only as crayon: the sign and the paper swatches cropped from it. Forthcoming is a drafting revision mark, not a coloured badge. Outlined square icon buttons.

STORY: The visitor meets the sign and knows it is a person; reads one line of role; reads the about text and sees two photos; then scans a cut list of papers under Scalable Governance, opens one for authors, venue, abstract, link; sees the exploratory AI-in-research strand; leaves via contact icons.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Sticky header with About · Research · Contact right and an empty name slot left. The sign centred at about half the viewport width, untouched. Below it one line "PhD researcher in AI governance at TU Munich." and three outlined icon buttons (email, LinkedIn, Google Scholar). On desktop at 1440x900 the ink crayon stroke and the first lines of the about text are visible below the hero, as in the chosen min-c sketch.

FORM: Shop Drawing (grounded candidate 4), minimal variant C "cut list", chosen by the user over title-block and datum variants. Signature interactions (user-requested): as the hero scrolls away, the sign's 14 panels lift off column by column with a slight hand-placed tilt and reassemble as a small sign in the header's name slot, cut along the drawing's real grid lines and each lifting before it reaches the header (reduced motion: the small sign fades in; no JS: text name). Scroll emerging (user: "elements popping up/emerging when scrolling down"): headings, intros, closed part rows and photos rise 28px and unblur over the first 96px of entering view, scroll-driven, never on opened content or the first viewport. Photos (user: "expand a bit on mouse over and be able to click on them for a more detailed inspection"): hover grows the photo slightly and steps its frame from the hair to the heavy pen; click opens a native dialog on near-opaque film with a heavy ink frame and square close button, no captions (user: "no cringe photo subtitles"). The hero sits on a heavier sheet of paper grain (balanced dark and light specks so its tone stays #f3f5f3) that fades into the page; the header is clear over it at rest, takes the same paper once the hero reaches it, and its normal paper once the sign docks (user: "stronger paper texture").
