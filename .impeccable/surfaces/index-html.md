# Surface: home (index.html)

Scope: the whole one-page site (hero → about → photos → research → footer); Imprint, Privacy and 404 inherit the frame. Visitor mode: Read. Audience, content and constraints live in PRODUCT.md.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is the shop drawing for something being built, reduced to its cut list. The hand-drawn sign is the only painted plate; everything else is graphite linework on drafting film. Refuses the academic template (serif name, cream, bullet publication list) and the corporate claim-and-proof page.

OWN-WORLD: Cool drafting-film ground (#f3f5f3) carrying a very subtle paper grain (user request, to match the crayon), graphite ink, no grid paper, no dimension lines, no title block. Two pens only: heavy 2px (table openers, icon buttons, toggles) and 1px hair (row dividers, photo frames). Narrow drafting caps (Archivo Narrow) for labels, part numbers and venues; a plain grotesk (Hanken Grotesk) for reading. Sign colours appear only as state: an opened paper fills its part-number cell with one panel colour from the sign. Forthcoming is a drafting revision mark, not a coloured badge. Outlined square icon buttons.

STORY: The visitor meets the sign and knows it is a person; reads one line of role; reads the about text and sees two photos; then scans a cut list of papers under Scalable Governance, opens one for authors, venue, abstract, link; sees the exploratory AI-in-research strand; leaves via contact icons.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Sticky header with About · Research · Contact right and an empty name slot left. The sign centred at about half the viewport width, untouched. Below it one line "PhD researcher in AI governance at TU Munich." and three outlined icon buttons (email, LinkedIn, Google Scholar). On desktop at 1440x900 the 2px rule and the first lines of the about text are visible below the hero, as in the chosen min-c sketch.

FORM: Shop Drawing (grounded candidate 4), minimal variant C "cut list", chosen by the user over title-block and datum variants. Signature interactions (user-requested): as the hero scrolls away, the sign's 14 panels lift off column by column with a slight hand-placed tilt and reassemble as a small sign in the header's name slot (reduced motion: the small sign fades in; no JS: text name).
